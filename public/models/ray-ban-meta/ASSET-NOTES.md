## Runtime adaptation by Glance

The inverse frame transform removes the export’s shared translation, yaw, and uniform scale. Relative mesh transforms and all geometry are preserved. No mesh simplification, deformation, anisotropic scale, or texture resampling is applied. Existing moderate texture sizes and the compact GLB do not warrant detail loss.

The normalized frame faces +Z and its temples extend toward -Z. The wearer’s right lens is local -X (viewer-left in a frontal product view). Its measured bounds are X [-1.941477, -0.369414], Y [-0.635987, 0.570879], Z [-0.069353, -0.016231]. Its center drives the camera alignment. The preview is a separate copy of that lens’s inward-facing triangles, preserving the real silhouette and curvature.

Glance adjusts black-frame roughness/clearcoat, subtle lens tint and opacity, camera-lens reflections, framing, and lighting. Transmission render passes are disabled in favor of restrained alpha transparency; original logo textures and alpha channels are retained. Materials are cloned, so the cached source remains unchanged. The model loads with the lazy hero, reuses cached geometry on replay, and stops continuous rendering after entry/offscreen. A failed asset load can be retried while the photo demos remain usable.
